{D3750}]@##/_/rgya gar skad du/_dzam+b+ha la sto tra/_
[\[85a\]]
[\[85a.1\]]bod skad du/_dzam+b+ha la'i bstod pa/_dzam+b+ha la la phyag 'tshal lo/_/lha dang lha min dri za mi'am ci rnams kyi/_/cod pan 'od kyis zhabs kyi khri ni gsal mdzad pa/_/byang chub mchog tu nges mdzad gnod sbyin dbang phyug la/_/sems can rnams la sman pa'i bsam 
[\[85a.2\]]pas bdag gis gsol/_/gang zhig brtse ba'i bdag nyid byang chub sbyin mdzad khyod/_/snying rje'i rtsa ba sems can don du rab tu grags/_/mgon khyod sems can rnams la brtse bar mi mdzad na/_/khyod kyis sangs rgyas dran par gang gi 'gyur te 'tshal/_/shin tu mi bzad 
[\[85a.3\]]dbul ba'i sdug bsngal nang du ni/_/'gro rnams lhung ba khyod kyis mthong bar ma gyur tam/_/rin chen gter chen rnams kyi mtshan nas bzung byas nas/_/khyod kyis chu bo yang ni rdzing gis bcing bar mdzod/_/shin tu dbul ba'i byin za mi bzad drag po yis/_/'gro ba gang zhig 
[\[85a.4\]]nyin dang mtshan du sreg par byed/_/brtse ba nyid kyis ser sna rnam par spong mdzod la/_/rin chen bdud rtsi'i char gyi char rgyun drag tu phob/_/khyod ni rgod pas brgya byin gyis ni rgyal srid dang /_/spyan zur tsam gyis khyab 'jug gis ni dpal mo dang /_/mnyes pas dbang phyug 
[\[85a.5\]]gis ni gangs ri'i gnas kyang thob/_/mgon po sdug phongs 'gron bu tsam yang mi ster ram/_/'gro ba'i dbang phyug rnams la dpag med bde ba rnams/_/rtag tu khyod ster sdug bsngal can la de lta min/_/khyod kyis sngon ma mthong ba snying rje'i yul gyur pa'i/_/dbul ba'i sdug 
[\[85a.6\]]bsngal can la ji ltar khyod 'jigs sam/_/nor gyis nor slong gus ldan rnams la khyod 'gengs kyi/_/yang dag skal med sdig byas rnams la khyod min zhing /_/nor gtong nor gyis nor ldan par ni 'dzin gyur te/_/mgon po slong ba'i tshogs rnams nges par spong byed dam/_/gal te 
[\[85a.7\]]khyod kyis nor gtong rnams la khyod kyis ster/_/skye bo tshig ngan rnams la ji ltar khyod 'jigs sam/_/rab tu 'bad pas nges par smra ba de yis mchog_/gnod sbyin rgyal po bdag gis gsol ba gsan du gsol/_/rab tu 'bad byas de 'dra'i nor dang ldan gyur nas/_/ji ltar 
[\[85b\]]
[\[85b.1\]]dbul po'i sgra ni thos par ma gyur cig_/dbul po'i me ni rab tu 'bar bas 'gro ba rnams/_/sreg par nges mthong yid kyis ngo mtshar nyid du gyur/_/gang zhig bdag gis smras pa'i tshig rtsub tshig rnams ni/_/chu yi dbang pos brtse phyir 'di rnams bzod par mdzod/_/gang zhig 
[\[85b.2\]]bstod pa 'di yis ni/_/rtag tu 'bad pas dzaM la bstod/_/des ni dbul por mi 'gyur zhing /_/shin tu mi g.yo'i dpal thob shog_/mi'i dbang po nyi ma rnam par gnon pas dzam+b+ha la la bstod pa mdzad pa rdzogs so//_//kal+yA Na mi tra gzhon nu shes rab kyis bskul nas/_
